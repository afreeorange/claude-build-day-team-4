---
title: GLP-1 medication safety facts (patient Medication Guides)
source: FDA-approved Medication Guides for Wegovy (semaglutide) and Zepbound (tirzepatide)
status: DRAFT, pending clinician review
---
These medications are prescription medicines. Your prescriber decides your dose and schedule. Questions about doses, missed doses, increasing or decreasing, or stopping the medication should always go to your prescriber or pharmacist.

The Medication Guides list possible serious side effects that patients should know about, including: inflammation of the pancreas (pancreatitis), gallbladder problems, low blood sugar (especially when also taking insulin or a sulfonylurea), kidney problems often related to dehydration, serious allergic reactions, changes in vision for people with diabetes, a warning about possible thyroid tumors, and depression or thoughts of suicide (you should watch for mood changes).

Low blood sugar signs can include dizziness or light-headedness, blurred vision, anxiety, irritability or mood changes, sweating, slurred speech, hunger, confusion, shakiness, a fast heartbeat, and feeling jittery.

Tell your care team before any surgery or procedure that you are taking a GLP-1 medication, because these medicines slow stomach emptying, which matters for anesthesia.
