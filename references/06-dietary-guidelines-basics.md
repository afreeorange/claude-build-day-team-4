---
title: Healthy eating pattern basics
source: Dietary Guidelines for Americans (dietaryguidelines.gov)
status: DRAFT, pending clinician review
---
A healthy eating pattern includes vegetables of all types, fruits (especially whole fruits), grains with at least half being whole grains, dairy or fortified soy alternatives, a variety of protein foods (lean meats, poultry, eggs, seafood, beans, peas, lentils, nuts, seeds, soy), and oils.

The Guidelines recommend limiting foods and drinks higher in added sugars, saturated fat, and sodium, and limiting alcohol.

Water is the best choice for staying hydrated. Sugar-sweetened drinks add calories without much nutrition.

A healthy eating pattern can be adapted to personal preferences, culture, and budget. Every food choice is an opportunity to move toward a healthier pattern.
