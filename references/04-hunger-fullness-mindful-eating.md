---
title: Hunger and fullness cues and mindful eating
source: Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics consumer guidance (eatright.org) on mindful eating; lifestyle medicine program materials
status: DRAFT, pending clinician review
---
A hunger-fullness scale from 1 to 10 can help you notice your body's signals: 1 is ravenous, 5 is neutral, and 10 is uncomfortably stuffed. Many people aim to start eating around 3–4 (gently hungry) and stop around 6–7 (comfortably satisfied).

On GLP-1 medications, fullness often arrives sooner and more strongly than before. Pausing halfway through a meal to check in with your body can help you stop before you feel sick.

Eating can be driven by emotions, stress, boredom, or habit as well as by physical hunger. Noticing the difference without judgment is a skill that grows with practice. Before eating outside of meals, it can help to pause and ask "What am I feeling right now? What do I need?"

Long-term change is built from small, repeatable habits, not perfection. A "bad" day is information, not failure.
