---
title: Managing common stomach side effects on GLP-1 medications
source: FDA-approved patient Medication Guides for Wegovy (semaglutide) and Zepbound (tirzepatide); 2025 joint nutrition advisory (ACLM/ASN/OMA/TOS)
status: DRAFT, pending clinician review
---
The most common side effects of GLP-1 medications are nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, constipation, stomach pain, indigestion or reflux, and feeling tired. They are most common when starting the medication or after a dose increase, and often improve over time.

Food strategies that many people find helpful:
- Eat smaller meals and stop when you first feel full.
- Eat slowly and avoid lying down right after eating.
- Limit fried, greasy, very rich, or very sweet foods, which often worsen nausea.
- Bland foods (crackers, toast, rice, broth) may be easier on nausea days.
- For constipation: drink fluids throughout the day, increase fiber gradually (vegetables, fruit, beans, whole grains), and keep moving.
- Sip fluids regularly, especially if you have vomiting or diarrhea, to avoid dehydration.

Never change, skip, or adjust your medication dose on your own. Talk with your prescriber about side effects; they decide whether any changes are needed.

Contact your care team right away if you have: severe stomach pain that will not go away (with or without vomiting, sometimes spreading to the back); vomiting or diarrhea that keeps you from keeping fluids down; signs of dehydration such as dizziness or very little urine; pain in the upper right belly, fever, or yellowing of the skin or eyes; or signs of a serious allergic reaction. Call 911 in an emergency.
